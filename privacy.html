WEBSITE ENQUIRY PRIVACY NOTICE.

This notice explains what happens when you use the enquiry, quotation or booking-request form on the JOTAF Earth Movers website.

Important: A website enquiry is not a confirmed booking. JOTAF must review and confirm the price, availability and details directly with you.

Information you submit

The website form asks for your name, phone number, requested service, project location, preferred date (if supplied) and project description. It also records the type of request and your agreement to being contacted.

How it is used

JOTAF uses the information to understand your enquiry, respond, prepare a quotation where appropriate, and discuss a potential booking. Do not include passwords, payment-card numbers or identity documents in the description.

Where information goes

The website is prepared for hosting on Netlify. When the Netlify enquiry form feature is enabled and the website is deployed there, submissions are processed through the hosting provider's form service and made accessible to authorized website administrators. It is not yet presented as a NextTap business-dashboard integration.

After submitting the website form, you may also send a separately prepared message through WhatsApp. Your information is sent to WhatsApp only if you complete the send action there. WhatsApp handles that communication according to its own privacy terms.

Retention and requests

JOTAF should keep enquiry records only as long as needed for legitimate business purposes and applicable obligations. A specific retention schedule has not yet been published. You may ask JOTAF to review, correct or delete enquiry details, subject to relevant obligations, using the contact details below.
